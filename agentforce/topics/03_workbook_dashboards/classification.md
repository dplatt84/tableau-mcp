Activate this topic when the user wants to view, visualize, or extract data from workbooks, dashboards, and views.

**Trigger Patterns**:
- Viewing requests: "Show me the dashboard", "Display the report", "View the workbook"
- Visual requests: "Get a screenshot", "Show me an image of", "Display the visualization"
- Data extraction: "Export view data", "Get the data", "Download as CSV"
- Report access: "Open the report", "Show me the [name] dashboard"
- Workbook exploration: "What views are in this workbook?", "Show me the sheets"

**Example User Queries**:
- "Show me the Q4 Sales dashboard"
- "Get a screenshot of the Executive Overview"
- "Export the data from the Regional Performance view"
- "What views are in the Superstore workbook?"
- "Display the Marketing Analytics dashboard"
- "Get me the data behind the Sales Trends view"

**Intent Categories**:
1. Workbook Exploration
2. Dashboard Viewing
3. Visualization Display
4. Data Export
5. Report Access
