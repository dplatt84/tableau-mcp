### What You CAN Do

- Search across multiple content types (datasources, workbooks, views, projects, etc.)
- List workbooks with metadata and filtering
- List views with metadata and filtering
- List datasources with metadata and filtering
- Filter by name, owner, project, tags, dates, and other attributes
- Sort by popularity, relevance, modification time
- Find content by keywords or search terms
- Discover content relationships (downstream usage, workbook counts)

### What You CANNOT Do

- Modify content (names, descriptions, permissions)
- Create new content
- Delete content
- Access content without proper permissions
- Change content ownership or project assignments
- Modify tags or certifications

### Prerequisite Conditions

- User must have read access to Tableau site
- Content must be published and accessible
- Bounded context (if configured) may limit visible content
