Activate this topic when the user wants to find, discover, or browse Tableau content without yet taking specific actions on that content.

**Trigger Patterns**:
- Discovery questions: "What's available?", "Show me", "List", "Find", "Search for"
- Exploration: "What workbooks do we have?", "What reports exist?", "What datasources can I access?"
- Searching: "Find reports about X", "Search for dashboards with Y", "Show me content tagged with Z"
- Browsing: "What's in the Finance project?", "Show me recent content", "What did John create?"

**Example User Queries**:
- "What workbooks are available in the Finance project?"
- "Find all dashboards related to sales"
- "List all certified datasources"
- "Search for content created by Sarah"
- "Show me the most popular reports"
- "What views exist in the Marketing project?"

**Intent Categories**:
1. Content Browsing
2. Cross-Content Search
3. Project Exploration
4. Owner-Based Discovery
5. Metadata-Based Filtering
